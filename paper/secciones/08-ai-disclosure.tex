\section*{AI Usage Disclosure}
\label{sec:ai-disclosure}

Generative artificial intelligence tools were used as supporting tools during the
development of this work. Their use was primarily focused on exploratory analysis,
literature review, methodological discussion, and editorial support. In the
literature review, AI tools were used to locate relevant information within papers,
identify reported metrics and results, compare methodological approaches across
studies, identify limitations explicitly stated by the authors, and suggest
potential connections between related works. AI assistance was also used to
organize bibliographic information and support the preparation of citations and
references.

AI tools were additionally used to support the preliminary examination of the
project datasets, including the identification of relevant variables, possible
data-quality issues, and considerations regarding feature availability and data
preprocessing. In the development of the research approach, AI was used to discuss
alternative machine-learning formulations, evaluation strategies, and
methodological considerations relevant to the prediction problem. Where
appropriate, AI assistance was also used to propose or review small scripts for
verification tasks, such as checking dataset properties or document consistency,
and to identify possible inconsistencies between different sections of the project
documentation.

The use of AI did not replace the authors' analysis or academic judgment. AI-
generated interpretations, methodological suggestions, bibliographic information,
and technical observations were treated as preliminary assistance rather than
authoritative evidence. Similarly, references identified indirectly through another
publication were treated as secondary citations and independently reviewed its
accuracy and potential value to our reasearch.

All relevant AI-assisted content was subsequently reviewed by the authors.
Bibliographic references and reported results were checked against their
corresponding sources, dataset-related observations were manually verified, and
methodological suggestions were evaluated according to the objectives and
constraints of the project. The authors retain full responsibility for the accuracy
of the final document, the correctness of its citations, the interpretation of the
literature, the methodological decisions, the experimental results, and the
conclusions presented in this work.
